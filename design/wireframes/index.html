New look and feel: wireframes, version 2

Version 2, 27 Nov 2026, with your answers built in: classes follow the licence, moving pictures on cards, English by default, default team names the school can change, a realistic simulation, calibration and login on the phone, and a new School view. Look C, Studio, is chosen for every screen; A and B stay for reference. Every screen is clickable. Nothing here is built into the product yet. The screens use the purchased Interactive Wall flow (9-dot calibration, IR pen, phone remote) with our JSON activity templates.

All music and sounds are made by the page itself, so they are ours to use.

Preschool quiz

Turn on music and sound effects at the top to hear it. One question, three giant answers, a voice prompt. Classes play as two teams, so there is no child data and everyone joins in.

  • Wrong answer: a gentle shake, no red cross
  • Right answer: confetti and praise, then Next
  • Peacocks and Tigers are the defaults. A school can rename them in the School view (try it there)

Simulation, Class 10

Rebuilt to look like a real lab bench: battery pack, a colour-coded resistor, a glass bulb and an analogue ammeter. Electrons flow faster as the current rises.

  • The resistor's colour bands change with its value, as on a real one
  • The graph plots each reading, so the class sees the straight line of Ohm's law
  • Push the current too high and the bulb warns it will burn out
DecidedReal-looking simulations; the school picks what it wants.
